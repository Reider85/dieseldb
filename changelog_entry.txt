3.0.68 Prompt 35 - eliminate O(n^2) delete via deferred bulk-update window (beginBulkUpdate/endBulkUpdate)

Deferred bulk-update mode in the delimited storage layer: RowStorage gains no-op begin/endBulkUpdate, AbstractRowStorage delegates to the index manager and routes syncIndexBulk through markIndexDirty, and DelimitedIndexManager accumulates deleteRow/insertAt changes on its mirror rows list during the window (no per-op rowId assignment, tombstone marking, or rebuild), closing with a single reindex() when dirty. Table.copyForTransaction now mirrors via one setRows call inside a window (was a per-row insertAt loop), Table.compact wraps setRows in begin/end, and DeleteQuery.execute wraps the whole deletion in beginBulkUpdate()/endBulkUpdate() so DELETE WHERE rebuilds the index once instead of once per deleted row.
